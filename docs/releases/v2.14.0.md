## Scope

- An account that Google wants verified before Antigravity can use it now shows `VERIFY`, in the TUI and in `list`/`limits`, instead of staying pending. The state is saved with the account and clears after a successful refresh.
- `w` in the TUI and the new `agy-swap verify <account>` command open Google's verification page in the system browser without copy/paste. Only `https` pages on `google.com` or its subdomains are opened.
- `add` checks the new account once after saving it. When Google wants it verified, it says so, prints the link and offers to open it.
- TUI: after adding an account (`a`), quota refreshes immediately. An account needing verification no longer says "Needs re-auth" or suggests `agy-swap add`.
- Security: Builds and `go install` require Go 1.27.3, addressing GO-2026-6809.
- Site: Added `verify` command to command registry; added `desc.verify` and `VERIFY` status word across English, Thai, Japanese, and Chinese; updated Go requirement floor to 1.27.3; bumped story frame version.
