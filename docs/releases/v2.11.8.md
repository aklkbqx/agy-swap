## Scope

- [Next #38](https://github.com/aklkbqx/agy-swap/issues/38): under the `balanced` policy, `agy-swap next` and the TUI `n` key ranked accounts by remaining quota without excluding the active one, so the fullest active account "rotated" to itself and reported success. `next` now takes the best ready account other than the active one and keeps the active account only when no other account is ready. `recommend` and bound runs are unchanged. Found by the rotation review of the [v2.11.7](v2.11.7.md) bug hunt.
- Site: version alignment only.

## Candidate verification
