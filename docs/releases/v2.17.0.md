## Scope

- Pointer interaction (#75): one hit map per frame for accounts, the divider and stacked rule, scroll bars, footer key hints, dialog buttons and palette rows. Hover shows what a click does without moving the selection; a click beside a modal closes it; OSC 22 pointer shapes on kitty, foot, Ghostty and WezTerm; `AGY_SWAP_MOUSE=0` turns the mouse off.
- Layout (#74): lists and gauge panels that do not fit scroll with a bar instead of hiding accounts; the stacked rule drags within the range that changes what shows; every page keeps a blank row above and below its content; the quota overview page is removed.
- Live quota sync (#76): the account in use every 20 s, the others every 2 min, reads right after a reset and after Antigravity writes to its logs; a 15-minute project memo makes most reads one request; per-account back-off on HTTP 429 and 5xx; reset times corrected for clock skew; snapshots no longer skipped for a round.
- Site: quota copy in English, Thai, Japanese and Chinese gives the new cadence and no longer mentions the overview page; story frames regenerated.
