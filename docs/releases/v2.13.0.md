# v2.13.0 delivery evidence

Owner approved v2.13.0 publication and deployment on 2026-10-18. The [Release](https://github.com/aklkbqx/agy-swap/releases/tag/v2.13.0) was published at 2026-10-18T08:29:11Z with six platform binaries and checksums. Annotated immutable tag `v2.13.0` is on commit `c901582`, the merge of [PR #58](https://github.com/aklkbqx/agy-swap/pull/58). A matching immutable Site tag was published.

## Scope

- The TUI dashboard is rebuilt around the two quota pools (Gemini and Claude/GPT): one meter per pool and a status word per account, a detail side with one gauge per pool (digits, bar, reset time), and accounts that need attention grouped under their own divider with the fix named for NO TOKEN rows.
- The dashboard uses the whole window: digits grow up to twice their size, bars get thicker, the list gets wider with longer bars, and small windows fall back step by step to plain limit lines down to 28 by 12.
- The list/detail split resizes with the mouse (drag the divider) or with `[` and `]`; clicking an account selects it.
- `AGY_SWAP_ASCII` draws the dashboard with ASCII characters only; it is chosen automatically on a Linux console, a dumb terminal or a non-UTF-8 locale.
- Per-screen key hints, logo and credit on the welcome and help screens, warnings in yellow.
- Site: the story frames and legend show the new dashboard, and the social preview image was regenerated from the new hero.

## Verification

- `make qa` (tests, race detector, vet, lint, tui-smoke at nine window sizes) and `make release-check` passed. One race-run failure of a Homebrew stub test under heavy machine load did not reproduce alone or on a full re-run.
- `make release-assets` ran from tag `v2.13.0` with build ID `release-v2.13.0`; `make vuln` found nothing. GitHub asset digests match `checksums.txt`.
- Homebrew tap updated; `/opt/homebrew/bin/agy-swap` reports `agy-swap v2.13.0`, SHA-256 equal to the darwin/arm64 asset.
- Site published; `make site-verify-live VERSION=2.13.0` matched production byte for byte and the live site reports v2.13.0.

## Limits

- Mouse dragging and clicking were covered by tests of the geometry, not exercised in every terminal emulator.
- A terminal cannot change its font; "responsive" scaling is done with scaled block digits and thicker bars, so the look depends on the terminal's cell shape.
- Light terminal themes were not specifically reviewed.
