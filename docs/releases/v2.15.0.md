## Scope

- Google picks the quota groups it reports from the client version in the User-Agent. agy-swap now sends `antigravity/hub/2.23.0 <os>/<arch>`, which brings back the Gemini weekly limit (#50) and adds the Gemini 4 Argon weekly pool. `AGY_SWAP_USER_AGENT` overrides it.
- Argon is its own `argon` pool, shown in `list`, `limits`, a TUI gauge panel and a quota-overview column. `--family argon` picks accounts by it, or by the Gemini pool on an account without one (Ultra).
- Without a family, readiness, the status word and the statusline minimum judge the shared Gemini and Claude/GPT pools, so a used-up Argon pool does not bench an account.
- Pool ids and limit windows are validated by shape, so pools and windows Google adds later are kept.
- TUI: side-by-side pool panels sit two to a row; a weekly-only pool is never shown as a paused 5-hour limit.
- Site: legend copy for Argon in English, Thai, Japanese and Chinese; story frames regenerated.

## Limits

- The client version is a fixed constant. If Google raises its threshold again, pools disappear until agy-swap is updated or `AGY_SWAP_USER_AGENT` is set.
- On an account without an Argon pool, agy-swap cannot tell an Ultra account from one that has no Argon access; `--family argon` reads the Gemini pool for both.
- G1 credits and reading quota through `agy -p /usage` are not covered.
